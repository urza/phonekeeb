How to type

Start in the center circle. Keep your finger on screen and sweep to one of the segments (up, left, down or right). Still keeping your finger down sweep across the lines to neighbouring sector and come back to center. The letter's distance from the center tells you how many lines to cross. Letters sit on the side where your sweep starts. A letter's color names the region your sweep returns from. Space: tap the center, or dip out and back without crossing a line. Double-tap the center for a period. One extra full loop makes a capital. Tap an area: right deletes, bottom is enter. Hold right and drag toward the center to delete more; drag back to restore. Hold top and drag sideways to move the caret. Drag from the bottom area: right types ?, up types ! (the center circle counts as up), left types a comma. The corner button copies the text, or only the selected part. Suggestions mix English and Czech, follow the sentence's language, and restore diacritics and apostrophes (tata → táta, dont → don't). The keyboard learns your words and phrases on this device only; the settings can switch that off or forget everything learned.
