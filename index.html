Start in the center circle. Sweep across the lines and come back. The letter's distance from the center tells you how many lines to cross. Letters sit on the side where your sweep starts, in the color of that region. Space: tap the center, or dip out and back without crossing a line. Double-tap the center for a period. One extra full loop makes a capital. Tap an area: right deletes, bottom is enter. Hold right and drag toward the center to delete more; drag back to restore. Hold top and drag sideways to move the caret.
